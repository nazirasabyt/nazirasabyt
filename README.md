### About me

I work at the intersection of data engineering and consumer market analysis. Based in Sharjah, I focus on UAE's hyper-saturated F&B and lifestyle markets — building systems that find real opportunities where most operators see only competition.

My current project: a market intelligence system that ingests data from 8 sources (Google Trends, Maps, TikTok, Instagram, customer reviews, and more), scores consumer concepts on a composite opportunity metric, and surfaces insights through interactive dashboards. The system tracks 10+ concepts across Sharjah, Dubai, and global benchmarks.

What I care about: **rigorous data, sharp questions, honest analysis**. I would rather find one true thing than ten plausible-sounding ones.

---

### What I build

**End-to-end data pipelines.** I design systems that move data from raw API responses through cleaned tables to scored business insights — using Python, DuckDB, dbt, and Streamlit. Reliability and reproducibility are non-negotiable.

**Analytical scoring models.** I'm interested in how you turn messy real-world data into defensible numbers. My current scoring framework combines demand velocity, market saturation, customer sentiment, and operational feasibility into a single Opportunity Score that founders and operators can actually use.

**Decision-support dashboards.** Dashboards that surface answers, not just data. I obsess over what *not* to show — every chart needs to earn its place.

### How I work

I write detailed case studies of my analyses — not just code dumps. Each one walks through the question, method, finding, and limitations. Some examples on [my portfolio]([your-portfolio-url]):
